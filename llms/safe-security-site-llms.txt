# SAFE Security

> SAFE is an Autonomous Cyber Risk Management platform that unifies AI Security Posture Management (AI-SPM), Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM), Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM), and Cyber Risk Quantification (CRQ) on a single AI-native platform. SAFE helps CISOs, cyber risk officers, and third-party risk teams continuously identify, prioritize, and reduce cyber risk using agentic AI, turning fragmented security data into clear, defensible, dollar-based risk decisions.

SAFE was named a Leader in the Forrester Wave for Cyber Risk Quantification and a Visionary in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Exposure Assessment Platforms. Ten percent of the Fortune 500, including T-Mobile, Verizon, Delta, and Booz Allen Hamilton, use SAFE to manage AI, third-party, exposure, and strategic cyber risk. SAFE is built on the FAIR standard and delivered the industry's first fully autonomous TPRM and CTEM solutions.
